# Model provenance

- `rs_arm_upstream.xml`, `rs_grasp_scene_upstream.xml`: verbatim copies of
  `rebotarm_ros2/src/rebotarm_mujoco_rs/models/` in the public repository
  [Yang-Ci/ReBot_Arm_DigitalTwin_RS](https://github.com/Yang-Ci/ReBot_Arm_DigitalTwin_RS)
  (formerly `ReBot_Arm_web_RS`) at `d289fa7` (sha256 `94dd0542…` and `363132e2…`). That
  package is based on `LAN-GER/reBot-B601-RS-for-mujoco_sim` @ `1249cb6` with local gripper,
  material and badge edits.
- `meshes/` (not in git, 63 MB): the STL files referenced by `rs_arm_upstream.xml`, fetched from
  the same commit by `tools/fetch_meshes.sh` and checked against `meshes.sha256`.
- `b601_t1_scene.xml`: generated by `tools/build_scene.py` from the upstream arm,
  `configs/real_limits.json` and `configs/t1_layout.json`. Do not edit by hand.
- `tool_visual/` (display only, 16 STL files + `manifest.json`): the installed end effector drawn
  from the reBot CAD context of the real rig, simplified by vertex clustering and generated by
  `tools/build_tool_visuals.py`: R8 force-sensor adapter plates, printed fingertip connectors and
  the 30° D405 wrist mount (Bowen Zhu's designs), the PX6D force/torque sensor and DM-Tac W2L
  tactile fingers (the vendors' publicly downloadable CAD), an Intel RealSense D405 envelope, and
  the gripper body and M7 stator from the upstream meshes above. They have no contact or mass in
  the simulation; collisions use the primitive shapes in `tools/build_scene.py`.
